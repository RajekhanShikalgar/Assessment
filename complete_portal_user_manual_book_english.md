# Foreword & Academic Context

Under traditional higher education paradigms, academic assessment was predominantly limited to a single summative terminal examination conducted at the end of the semester. This restricted framework hindered comprehensive evaluation of students' consistent academic engagement, analytical competencies, verbal communication, research methodology, and practical application skills.

The **National Education Policy (NEP 2020)** and the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** guidelines have mandated **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)** as a core, credit-bearing assessment model that must be formative, transparent, diversified, and student-centric.

To address these requirements, **Prof. Dr. Rajekhan Shiraj Shikalgar** developed the **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) Management System (rajekhan.in)**. This cloud-based digital platform serves colleges, autonomous institutions, and university departments by transforming internal assessments into a 100% paperless, transparent, and auditable workflow.

# Part I: Foundational Framework & Key Highlights

### 1.1 Three-Tier Architecture
The platform operates on a synchronized three-tier institutional hierarchy:

| Tier | Institutional Role | Primary Responsibilities & Functions | Access Mode & Security |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | 🏛️ **Institutional Admin** | Faculty account approvals, validity renewals, master directory & notice board | Admin Username & Encrypted Password |
| **2** | 👨‍🏫 **Course Faculty** | Class roster enrollment, CIE mapping, session creation, evaluation & marks visibility | Unique **Teacher Code** + Private Password |
| **3** | 🎓 **Students** | Direct assignment submission, online quizzes, cloud PDF & video link verification | **Teacher Code + PRN** (Frictionless Zero-Password) |

---

### 1.2 Frictionless Zero-Password Access for Students
Managing separate passwords across multiple platforms frequently causes login failures, reset delays, and frustration among students.
- In this portal, **students do not need to create, remember, or reset any passwords**.
- A student connects instantly by providing the course teacher's unique **Teacher Code** (e.g., `TCH-GEO-01`) along with their official University **Permanent Registration Number (PRN / Roll No)**.
- The system verifies the student against the teacher's enrolled roster and grants immediate access to all active coursework.

---

### 1.3 HarfBuzz Devanagari Complex Text Shaping Engine
Generating academic reports in Indian regional languages often suffers from typography rendering bugs where conjunct consonants (जोडाक्षरे), matras, and diacritics break or misalign.
- The system embeds the international **HarfBuzz + FreeType** text shaping pipeline.
- All official score sheets, submission receipts, and consolidated grade matrices render flawless, publication-quality bilingual Devanagari typography.

# Part VI: Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### Q. 1: Do students require passwords to log in?
> **Answer:** No. Students do not need passwords. Access is granted instantly using the course teacher's unique **Teacher Code** and the student's official **PRN**.

#### Q. 2: Can students submit assignments using smartphones?
> **Answer:** Yes, 100%. The system is fully responsive across mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers. Students can type online or link their handwritten notes via Google Drive directly from their phones.

#### Q. 3: How do teachers keep marks withheld until the final departmental release?
> **Answer:** In the **"3. Assessment Sessions"** table on the Teacher Dashboard, click the **`👁️ Marks Visible`** button once. It immediately toggles to **`🔒 Marks Hidden`**, masking scores on student portals while keeping evaluations secure.

#### Q. 4: In group assignments, does every student have to submit individually?
> **Answer:** No. Only the designated **Group Leader** submits the assignment. The platform automatically synchronizes the submission across all enrolled group members.

#### Q. 5: How does the system handle high volumes of student file uploads without server crashes?
> **Answer:** The system uses a **Zero Server Storage Cloud Architecture**. Students store their PDF files, presentations, or videos on personal cloud drives (Google Drive / OneDrive / YouTube) and share the link. This prevents server bottlenecks and preserves student custody of their work.

#### Q. 6: How can teachers manage multiple classes and subjects?
> **Answer:** Under **"2. Subject Mapping"**, teachers can enroll as many subjects and classes as they teach. During assignment creation or score matrix generation, simply select the desired course from the dropdown.

#### Q. 7: What should a student do if an incorrect link or draft was submitted?
> **Answer:** The student should contact their course teacher. The teacher can click **"Reopen for Revision"** on the evaluation screen, immediately granting the student access to resubmit.
